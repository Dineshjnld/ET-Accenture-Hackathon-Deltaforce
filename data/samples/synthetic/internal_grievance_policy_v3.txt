=====================================================================
Fictional Internal Policy: Customer Grievance Redressal & Internal
Ombudsman Operations Manual v3.0 — Demo Metro Bank (fictional)
Effective: 2025-04-01 | Owner: Head of Customer Service
=====================================================================

1. Purpose
This fictional policy describes how Demo Metro Bank (a fictional scheduled
commercial bank with 120 banking outlets in India) operates its Internal
Ombudsman (IO) office. It is a SAMPLE for software demonstration and does not
create any legal obligation.

2. IO Appointment (Control CTL-IO-APPOINT)
The bank shall appoint at least one Internal Ombudsman. The Customer Service
Committee of the Board shall review the IO strength at least once a year.
Owner: Head of Customer Service. Frequency: annual review. Expected evidence:
Board CSC minutes and IO appointment letter. Testing: inspect the latest CSC
minutes and the appointment file.

3. Vacancy Reporting (Control CTL-IO-VACANCY)
The bank shall inform the Reserve Bank of India within 10 working days from
the date of any IO vacancy caused by resignation, incapacitation, illness or
death. Owner: Compliance Officer. Frequency: event-driven. Expected evidence:
RBI intimation letter with dispatch proof. Testing: trace a sample of vacancy
events to dispatch records.

4. Complaint Escalation (Control CTL-IO-ESCALATE)
All complaints rejected or only partly redressed by the internal grievance
machinery shall be escalated to the Internal Ombudsman before the customer is
informed of the final decision. Owner: Grievance Redressal Officer. Frequency:
ongoing. Expected evidence: escalation register with IO remarks. Testing:
re-perform a sample of 25 escalated complaints.

5. Record Retention (Control CTL-IO-RECORDS)
The bank shall retain IO-related records for at least 5 years from the date
of final disposal of the complaint. Owner: Operations Head. Frequency: ongoing.
Expected evidence: records retention log. Testing: inspect retention log.

6. Quarterly Reporting to the Board (Control CTL-IO-BOARDREP)
The IO office shall submit a quarterly report to the Customer Service
Committee of the Board covering complaints reviewed, decisions upheld or
rejected, and systemic issues observed. Owner: Internal Ombudsman Office.
Frequency: quarterly. Expected evidence: quarterly IO report acknowledged by
the CSC. Testing: inspect the last two quarterly reports.

7. Known incompleteness (intentional, for demo)
Control CTL-IO-TRAINING (staff awareness training on IO scheme) exists by name
only: no documented objective, activity, owner or frequency is defined in this
sample, and no evidence exists. This intentional gap lets the demo show a
genuine design gap and missing evidence.
