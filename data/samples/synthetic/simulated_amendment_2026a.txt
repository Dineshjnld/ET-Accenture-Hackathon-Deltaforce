Chapter II — Office of the Internal Ombudsman (simulated changes)

5. Appointment of Internal Ombudsman
(1) The IO shall either be a retired or serving officer, in the rank
equivalent to a General Manager in the regulated entity. The bank shall
complete the appointment process within 60 days of a vacancy arising.

6. Vacancy reporting (AMENDED — simulated)
In case of any vacancy in the office of the IO / Deputy IO due to reasons
such as resignation, incapacitation, illness, death, etc., the bank shall
inform the Reserve Bank of India within 5 working days from the date of such
vacancy (reduced from 10 working days) and shall appoint a new IO / Deputy IO
as per eligibility within 60 days.

7. Number of Internal Ombudsman / Deputy Internal Ombudsman
(1) Every bank shall appoint at least one IO.
(2) The Customer Service Committee of the Board of the bank shall determine,
at least once in every six months, whether additional IO capacity is required
(changed from once a year in this simulation).

8. Quarterly systemic-issue reporting (NEW — simulated)
(1) The IO shall submit a quarterly report on systemic issues to the Customer
Service Committee of the Board within 15 days of the end of each quarter.
(2) The bank shall publish the number of complaints reviewed by the IO in its
annual report.
